# The Rubbers and Giants


## Chapter 1: In the past ##
One day a few years ago in another world, there was an additional species named the Rubbers, which are a "Large" school eraser with a smile face or dots on it to make it alive usually having a green purple or no sleeve and instead of humans they had Giants, which are just humans but with a different name because of them being named by rubbers instead of naming themself meaning that Human is the scientific name and Giant is the normal. The appearance of Rubber City is a dirt floor with a bunch of Big Rocks that are used as the foundation of Rubber City, houses are built on top of the rocks. Life in Rubber City was normal for most rubbers, not much would happen.



## Chapter 2: War ##
War started to begin between the two species because of the difference in species and the giants were certain to win. Most of the rubbers were killed or lost and there was nowhere to evacuate to because of the lack of cities and population during that time, all hope was lost for the rubbers and the giants were just about to win.



## Chapter 3: Hero ##
When all hope was lost for the rubbers one rubber appeared to save the day, his name was Joe Bozeo, Joe Bozeo would fight the giants just enough to not kill them until they fled from battle because of the amount of injuries Joe had caused, causing the rubbers to win the war because of a single rubber. The rubbers would classify him as the hero of Rubber City, and civilisation would continue for the rubbers and giants peacefully. When rubbers wrote books about Joe Bozeo they wouldn't include him fighting the giants due to his actions never being seen but instead being noticed later, they instead say that he convinced the giants to flee.



## Chapter 4: Nephew ##
Joe Bozeo eventually found a giant as his owner and he was only seen when taken outside by his owner which is quite rare which means that someone would have to take his place, and someone else perfectly fits. Bob Bozeo is Joe Bozeo's nephew and decided to take the place of Joe Bozeo while he lives with his owner. Bob would proceed to live the life of Joe Bozeo without fully replacing him. Because of how close they looked nobody noticed that it was Bob instead of Joe and people continued to talk about him.
