About Rubbers

Some rubbers don't have any body parts but are still alive. Rubbers without faces are usually not alive. If a rubber is cut in half and both pieces have a face they will each become a new person despite having been the same individual at one point. Some rubbers without faces are still alive including those in the Cratel Bozeo and Rupti families. It is unknown why this occurs but for these to be alive they must have one for the Colzo family or two dots on top of their head for any other one except the Colour family. It seems the side the dots are closer to is the side they see from which can be seen in rubbers like Taco Bozeo Oreo Bozeo Bob Cratel and Joe Bozeo. Rubbers in the Colour family do not have these dots. If you find a rubber that seems to be half the average height it is either cut in half or part of the Colour family. Colour family is for some reason always a square rectange with one thin side like they were cut in half, however having no memory of being cut.

Rubber Breeding

Male rubbers have been seen to build a nest and wait on a rooftop for a female rubber to join them. This is often a long process which is why rubbers prefer having an owner rather than being wild. The average number of eggs that rubbers have is 22 to 27. Any more or less is okay but rubbers have been taught over generations that 25 is the ideal amount. The breeding process is unknown but is said to take about six hours on average. It is also said that there is no waiting time and rubbers immediately hatch from eggs the second they are exposed to sunlight. If the eggs are not in sunlight they take around 5 to 30 minutes to hatch.

Rubber Taming

The taming process is usually easy as all you need to do is feed the rubber and take it into your house to care for it. If you would like to let your rubber be wild again all you have to do is build it a nest and leave it outside. If you would like to give it a more memorable goodbye you could create a new sleeve for it to wear to attract other rubbers or provide a manual on what to do in case it has been home-raised or has forgotten how to survive in the wild.

Rubber Appearance

Rubbers are usually about 5 cm tall 2 cm wide and 1 cm thick depending on the type. Some are 2 cm tall 2 cm wide and 1 cm thick like the cut in half or unique family ones. Most rubbers are white but they can be any colour depending on their type or their owner's choice.

Bozeo Family

The Bozeo family mainly contains tall rubbers with the only different being patterns, sleeves and height. The Bozeo family was mainly popularised because of one of it's members 'Joe Bozeo' who saved Rubber City in a war.

Colour Family

The Colour family has rubbers who are less tall about the size as if they were cut in half. People in the Colour family are always a unique colour except if they're white because white is a reoccuring colour in the Colour family.

Colzo Family

The Colzo family is a family that appeared later in the timeline and is like the Colour Family but a little taller and they have a horizontal line in the middle. The Colzo family started with a rubber named Crummy and it expanded.

Cratel Family

The Cratel family mainly contains tall rubbers with the only different being patterns, sleeves and height. The Cratel family is not different to the Bozeo family other than the name of the family. The Cratel family was mainly popularised because of one of it's members 'Bob Cratel'.

Rupti Family

The Rupti family does not have a specific look and can be any size other than the Colour and Colzo family heights. The Rupti family is often what factory or unknown rubbers are put into if they do not fit the description of other familys.

Rubber Personalities & Pronouns
Lore Important Characters

Joe Bozeo

Personality
Joe Bozeo's personality is like just an average person but popular.

Pronouns
Joe Bozeo uses he/him pronouns.

Actions
Joe Bozeo has a massive impact on rubbers because he was one of those who helped win the Rubbers and Giants War. Joe Bozeo is also the mayor of Rubber City.

Other
Joe Bozeo is not a wild rubber, but instead one with an owner. Joe Bozeo is very famous among rubbers.




Taco Bozeo

Personality
Taco Bozeo's personality consists of curiosity, determination, and confusion.

Pronouns
Taco Bozeo uses they/them pronouns.

Actions
Taco Bozeo makes secret deals with Pinky Colour in the bushes to the side of Rubberyork. Taco Bozeo's deals with Pinky are not known to the public.

Other
Taco Bozeo is a rubber that has a spotted texture with a damaged green sleeve. Taco Bozeo is unaware that Pinky has a bad history; they believe that the deals are only for future benefit and hide them from their owner. Taco Bozeo is treated badly by their owner. Taco Bozeo does not know Pinky's past and only knows the present version they believe has done nothing wrong.




Bob Cratel

Personality
Bob Cratel's personality is extremely similar to Joe Bozeo's.

Pronouns
Bob Cratel uses he/him pronouns.

Actions
A while after Joe Bozeo became mayor, he gained a temporary owner, meaning that his cousin, Bob Cratel, had to take over as mayor secretly and pretend to be Joe Bozeo.

Other
Bob Cratel looks identical to Joe Bozeo. After he finished pretending to be him, he put on a lighter-coloured sleeve to be distinguishable from Joe Bozeo.




Redy Colour

Personality
Redy Colour is a very energetic person while still being reasonable. He is kind.

Pronouns
Redy Colour uses he/him pronouns.

Actions
After Joe Bozeo became mayor, a family of rubbers named the 'Colour' family appeared and spread, starting with Pinky. When it was Joe Bozeo's chance to confront Pinky for his bad deeds, he brought Redy with him.

Other
Redy Colour contributed to Joe Bozeo's speeches, often handing him papers or things to read. Redy Colour has a smiling face with his left eye shut and right eye open.




Pinky Colour

Personality
Pinky Colour attempts to stay calm under pressure. He is cryptic, tries to move away from important info and manipulates others into doing what's good in his view.

Pronouns
Pinky Colour uses he/him pronouns.

Actions
After Joe Bozeo became mayor, Pinky Colour appeared seemingly out of nowhere and created the Colour Family. Pinky Colour was part of the creation of Rubberyork.

Other
Pinky Colour was confronted by Joe Bozeo and Redy after he forced citizens of Rubber City to come to Rubberyork without permission. Pinky Colour makes hidden deals with Taco Bozeo in the bushes to the left of Rubberyork.
